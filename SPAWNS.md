# Touhou Mobs: where they spawn

About once every 0.8 seconds for each player, Touhou Mobs tries to spawn one group, 24 to 48 blocks away, of a mob picked by weight below. The spot must be in one of the mob's biomes and pass its light rule. At most 12 of these mobs can be within 64 blocks of a player. On peaceful none spawn. They disappear when no player is within 128 blocks, and may wander off beyond 32, as monsters do.
